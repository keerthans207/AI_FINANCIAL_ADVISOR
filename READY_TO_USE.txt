================================================================================
  TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Server won't start?
  - Check port 8010 is available
  - Ensure model file exists in models/

Slow response?
  - Normal for first request (30-60s)
  - Subsequent requests: 3-6 seconds
  - Close other apps to free RAM

Out of memory?
  - Close unnecessary applications
  - You need 2GB+ free RAM
